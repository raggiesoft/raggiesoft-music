Carnaby Street

Fractured Prisms

The masterpiece that defined an era. Blending atmospheric progressive rock with sharp, melodic pop sensibilities, this 1983 release cemented Fractured Prisms as architects of the modern rock landscape. Named in honor of the 'Missing Four,' the title commemorates the final day the six family members spent shopping for records in London, just twenty-four hours before the Shap Fell disaster.
