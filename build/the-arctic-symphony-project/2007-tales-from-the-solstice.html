Tales from the Solstice

The 2007 3-Disc Symphonic Holiday Rock Opera

The Arctic Symphony Project
Narrative Release: November 13, 2007 • Real-World DSP Release: 2026-10-16 • Sound recording: ℗ 2026 Engine Room Records (Created by Michael P. Ragsdale)

Two survivors. Ten years apart. One unyielding rescue beacon.

Originally forged on December 11, 1996 in Ewell, Virginia, "Upon This Winter's Night" was recorded in a spare bedroom by eighteen-year-old twins Gabriel and Gwendolyn Rowlands and fourteen-year-old identical twins Maria and Clara Harrison when their non-verbal, autistic brother Julian "Julep" Rowlands was abducted by his estranged father. Built around Julian's wind-up Brahms Wiegenlied music box and Maria's silver glockenspiel, the track was hijacked across the Shenandoah FM broadcast repeaters by Richard Harrison—reaching Julian in a speeding car in West Virginia and giving him the strength to trigger a silent alarm at a gas station in Athens County, Ohio.

When Vanguard Horizon signed the family band in December 1996, executive Elias Thorne locked their Cymraeg ("Ar Noson y Gaeaf Hon"), Québécois French ("En Cette Nuit d'Hiver"), and Trilingual ("The Family Mix") recordings in the corporate vault, releasing only the English single in 1997. Years later, that 1997 recording became the "Sonic Armor" that grounded nineteen-year-old Matthew during his rescue from a cross-country abduction to Seattle. In January 2007, at The Crown & Hound on Duke of Gloucester Street in Williamsburg, Samantha and Jyn handed the Vanguard Horizon masters back to Julian and his family—commissioning the 3-disc symphonic rock opera Tales from the Solstice to tell Matthew's story and finally liberating Julian's vaulted Trilingual "Family Mix" for the world.

Studio Archives & Liner Notes

Behind-the-scenes production logs, engineering notes, and historical context from the Engine Room Records vault

The 1996 Shenandoah FM Broadcast Hijack

Engineered by Uncle Richard Harrison across the Blue Ridge repeater network on the night of December 11, 1996 so Julian would hear his siblings' voices and his Brahms music box melody through the car radio in the middle of a winter blizzard.

The Liberation of "The Family Mix" (Trilingual Finale)

Disc 3 culminates in the uncut 5-minute Trilingual "Family Mix" uniting Cymraeg (Welsh), Québécois French, and US English—honoring the Rowlands and Harrison heritage after a decade locked inside Vanguard Horizon's vault.

Tales from the Solstice — The Arctic Symphony Project

A sweeping 3-disc symphonic rock opera uniting two survivors across a decade. Originally forged on December 11, 1996 in Ewell, Virginia, 'Upon This Winter's Night' was recorded in a spare bedroom and broadcast across the Appalachian radio towers by Gabriel and Gwendolyn Rowlands and twins Maria and Clara Harrison to guide their fourteen-year-old brother Julian ('Julep') home from his abduction to Ohio. Ten years later, after learning that their 1997 single served as the 'Sonic Armor' that grounded nineteen-year-old Matthew during his cross-country abduction from Norfolk to Seattle and rescue by Jyn, Samantha bought out Vanguard Horizon's vaults—commissioning the family to compose the 3-disc rock opera telling Matthew's cross-country ordeal and liberating Julian's vaulted Trilingual 'Family Mix' (Cymraeg, Québécois French, and US English).
