The Forger's Call

30th-Anniversary CPI & Blacksburg Archival Compilation (1986–2016) • Companion Anthology to Live at the Crucible

The Stardust Engine

For thirty years—from October 18, 1986 until Homecoming Weekend on October 15, 2016—the only way on Earth to hear 'Ignition: The Forger's Call' was to walk through the Ironstone gates of The Crucible in Blacksburg, Virginia on a Saturday afternoon in autumn. Under the historic $1.00 Perpetual Mutual Cross-License ('The Progress Street Redline') drafted at the Progress Street kitchen table by sixteen-year-old dual-enrolled prodigy Holly O'Connell, the band intentionally kept 'Ignition' off every studio album and commercial compilation for three decades so that it belonged exclusively to Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI) and the sixty-five thousand fans inside The Crucible.

To celebrate the 30th Anniversary of 'Ignition: The Forger's Call' alongside the release of Live at the Crucible (where the band and the 300-piece CPI Marching Forgers opened the May 21, 2016 concert with the song's first-ever live album performance), Engine Room Records opened the Blacksburg vault to assemble The Forger's Call—a 16-track 30th-Anniversary CPI & Blacksburg Archival Compilation (1986–2016) included inside the Live at the Crucible Box Set at the CPI Campus Bookstore and available separately.

Bookended by both historic October 1986 recordings of the stadium anthem—Track 1 ('Ignition: The Forger's Call', the official 1986 Crucible Stadium Master played as the CPI Forgers football team runs out of the tunnel onto the field, whose heavy 82 BPM kick drum inspired the student section in The Anvil to organically invent 'The Stomp') and Track 16 ('Ignition: The Forger's Call [Demo Take 3]', the original Maxell XLII-S 90 4-track garage cassette where the band vocalizes 'TING... BOOM!' to test where the foundry anvil strikes)—the fourteen archival cuts in between trace thirty years of life, love, sanctuary, and resilience across CPI and Progress Street.

Studio Archives & Liner Notes

Behind-the-scenes production logs, engineering notes, and historical context from the Engine Room Records vault

Tracks 1 & 16: The Two October 1986 Recordings of "Ignition: The Forger's Call" & The Birth of "The Stomp"

The compilation opens and closes with the two October 1986 recordings that started it all. Track 16 ('Demo Take 3') is the raw 4-track Tascam Portastudio cassette recorded in the unheated garage on Progress Street for the CPI Student Government Association contest, where Ryan, Cassidy, Evan, and Tyler vocalized 'TING... BOOM!' onto the tape because they didn't own a blacksmith's anvil yet. Track 1 ('Ignition: The Forger's Call') is the finished 82 BPM Crucible Stadium Master recorded for the stadium press box after winning the contest, replacing the vocalized placeholders with a real steel foundry anvil strike and Tyler Wright's chest-rattling kick drum—the exact beat to which the student section in The Anvil organically crafted 'The Stomp' over several successive home games.

Track 2: "This Side of Heaven" (September 1988 Blacksburg Move-In Anthem)

Recorded in September 1988 between Electric Color and Neon Hearts as a sunlit 126 BPM synth-rock tribute to freshman move-in week at CPI, 'This Side of Heaven' was kept out of Apex Records' hands so the label couldn't turn a song about Blacksburg sanctuary into a generic pop single. Distributed on campus cassettes at the CPI Campus Bookstore and played every semester on WUVT / campus radio, it became the generational move-in anthem for CPI students—including Jordan Sinclair in January 2015 (The Eleventh Child, Episode 3, Chapter 1), for whom unpacking in Suite 214/216 while 'This Side of Heaven' plays on his phone triggers a joyful emotional overflow upon realizing his mother's oppression is finally behind him and he is safe.

Thirty Years of CPI Landmarks & The 1986 $1.00 Perpetual Mutual Cross-License

Because 16-year-old Holly O'Connell redlined CPI's boilerplate Work-for-Hire contest form in October 1986 and negotiated the $1.00 Perpetual Mutual Cross-License ('The Progress Street Redline', celebrated in Track 7, 'One Dollar on the Kitchen Table'), The Stardust Engine secured a perpetual legal right to reference CPI landmarks across their catalog—from Commonwealth Green (Track 5), The Keystone Bridge (Track 6), and Copernicus Hall (Track 8) to The Foundry dining hall and Maroon & Molten Orange (Track 9), Bennett Hall / 'The Old Lady on the Hill' (Track 10), and The Furnace (Track 11).

The Forger's Call — The Stardust Engine

Released on CPI Homecoming Weekend (October 15, 2016) alongside Live at the Crucible—both as a companion anthology inside the Live at the Crucible 30th-Anniversary Box Set at the CPI Campus Bookstore and as a standalone archival compilation—The Forger's Call spans thirty years (1986–2016) of previously uncollected Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI) and Blacksburg vault recordings. Anchored at Track 1 by the official October 1986 Crucible Stadium Master of 'Ignition: The Forger's Call' (played over The Crucible PA system as the CPI Forgers football team charges onto the field) and closed at Track 16 by the original October 1986 Progress Street 4-track garage cassette ('Ignition: The Forger's Call [Demo Take 3]', where the band vocalizes 'TING... BOOM!' to test where the foundry anvil strikes), the 16-track compilation chronicles the band's entire Blacksburg collegiate and hometown history—from 1988 campus move-in anthem 'This Side of Heaven' and 1990 acoustic ballad 'Snowfall on Commonwealth Green' to 1992's 'One Dollar on the Kitchen Table', 1998's 'The Old Lady on the Hill' (Bennett Hall), and 2016's brass benediction 'Ad Astra, Per Aspera: Hymn for the Crucible'.
