Live at the Crucible

Recorded Live Inside The Crucible (May 21, 2016) • Released Homecoming Weekend (October 15, 2016)

The Stardust Engine

Thirty years after five teenagers in a Progress Street garage won a 1986 student contest with a raw cassette tape—and sixteen-year-old Holly O'Connell protected it with her famous $1.00 Perpetual Mutual Cross-License—The Stardust Engine returned home to Blacksburg on Saturday, May 21, 2016 to take the stage inside The Crucible, the 65,000-seat concrete-and-Ironstone fortress of Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI) football. There was no opening act—just CPI Alumni (Class of 1989) coming home to play a full two-hour career retrospective for their alma mater.

Designed from the ground up as a concert 'For Forgers, By Forgers,' Holly O'Connell used the family's corporate treasury to buy out the entire Field Level and Lower Bowl upfront, selling every Forger Nation Presale seat strictly AT COST (non-transferably locked to each buyer's CPI Poly Pass ID so scalper botnets couldn't touch a single floor seat). During the mid-show Our Own Frequency acoustic block—where Evan and Tyler left the stage so Ryan and Cassidy could perform their most private brother-and-sister songs alone on acoustic guitar and piano—Ryan introduced the block's centerpiece, 'Brother's Keeper,' by speaking candidly about the daily ADLs (Activities of Daily Living) Cassidy and the family perform for his T10 paralysis behind closed doors. Because managing those daily care routines across global time zones had grown harder on his body and the family as they aged, Ryan announced that the Reignition World Tour would be the band's final world tour—establishing the annual CPI Summer Festival in Blacksburg as a permanent hometown residency where Forger Nation can come home to them every summer.

Vault Exclusive: Recorded live inside The Crucible on May 21, 2016 with the 300-piece CPI Marching Forgers. Full multi-disc live album in production following the studio release of 2015's Reignition; the Grand Finale Encore ('Electric Color (Live at the Crucible)') is available for immediate streaming in The Listening Room and RaggieSoft Book Reader.

Studio Archives & Liner Notes

Behind-the-scenes production logs, engineering notes, and historical context from the Engine Room Records vault

Act I: The 3-Song Alma Mater Opening with the CPI Marching Forgers

Before May 21, 2016, the only way to hear 'Ignition: The Forger's Call' was to attend a CPI Home Football Game in person inside The Crucible. For the opening of Live at the Crucible, all 300 members of the CPI Marching Forgers ('The Engine Room' Kevlar snare battery and full brass line) take the field with the band for (1) the first-ever recorded live concert performance of 'Ignition: The Forger's Call', (2) the CPI Fight Song 'CPI Tech Triumph: The Marching Forgers', and (3) a massive brass-and-synth rendition of the band's traditional concert opener, 'The Stardust Engine', before the Marching Forgers exit the tunnel—seemingly for the night.

Act IV: The "Our Own Frequency" Acoustic Block, "Brother's Keeper" & The CPI Summer Festival Residency

In 2008, during the middle of their Burnout Hiatus, Ryan and Cassidy recorded Our Own Frequency in complete secrecy in the living room of the Blacksburg house with just an acoustic guitar, a piano, and two microphones—never touring it, simply uploading it on a Tuesday morning with the one-sentence note: 'We're still here, and we're okay.' In Act IV, Evan and Tyler leave the stage so brother and sister can share those deeply private songs live for the very first time ('Our Own Frequency', 'Brother's Keeper', and 'Safe Person'). Introducing the centerpiece 'Brother's Keeper'—an unflinchingly intimate look at how Cassidy and the family perform Ryan's daily ADLs (toileting, morning anti-atrophy leg stretches, and Cassidy sleeping on the outside edge of the bed pushed against the wall as a human bed-rail so Ryan doesn't roll off) while Ryan acts as her emotional anchor—Ryan explains why the physical toll of those daily routines on the road makes Reignition their final world tour, unveiling the annual CPI Summer Festival residency in Blacksburg.

The Sacred Rule of "Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible"

Although the studio master of 'Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible' premiered over the stadium PA between the 3rd and 4th quarters on October 8, 2004, The Stardust Engine never performed the song on a concert stage until Track 18 of this May 21, 2016 homecoming—honoring their permanent vow that 'Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible' will ONLY ever be played live when performing inside The Crucible.

Operation Anvil: The "Last Bus Home" False Ending & The 1:40 Reveal of "Electric Color"

At Track 23, the band staged 'Last Bus Home: Lights Out on Progress Street' as a convincing false ending—turning the stage switches off and plunging the stadium into darkness so the audience believed the two-hour show was over. Then, in the pitch dark, all 300 members of the CPI Marching Forgers marched back onto the stage to join the band for Track 24: a secret 5-minute-26-second 1980s AOR Stadium Rock reclamation of 'Electric Color' (protected all semester by Holly's $300,000 student escrow and Cassidy's 'Exercise 87-B' decoy charts). Because the 77-second AOR guitar and 300-horn brass overture sounds nothing like the 1987 Dance Synth-Pop single, not even fans in Section 104, Row A recognized the song until 1:40 on the dot, when Cassidy belted: 'Woke up this morning, the world was gray...'

For Forgers, By Forgers: The $15M Scalper Lockout

Under Addendum No. 3 to her 1986 Cross-License, Holly O'Connell walked away from an estimated $12M–$15M+ in dynamic-pricing stadium gross by buying out all ~35,000 Field Level and Lower Bowl seats upfront and selling them AT COST through CPI's Zero-Knowledge SAML 2.0 Poly Pass portal—protecting FERPA-locked students like Jordan Shawn Kelly while locking TicketSphere and SphereHub scalper botnets completely out of the lower bowl.

Live at the Crucible — The Stardust Engine

The band's most important live album. Recorded live on Saturday, May 21, 2016 inside The Crucible in Blacksburg, Virginia—Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute's 65,000-seat stadium and the loudest house in all of college football—and released commercially during CPI Homecoming Weekend on October 15, 2016. With no opening act, CPI Alumni (Class of 1989) return home for a two-hour, 24-track career-spanning stadium show marking the finale of the band's final world tour before launching their annual CPI Summer Festival residency in Blacksburg. Opens with the 300-piece CPI Marching Forgers ('Ignition: The Forger's Call', 'CPI Tech Triumph', and 'The Stardust Engine'), includes archival gems from Lost Sounds, an intimate acoustic block from Our Own Frequency, the live concert debut of 'Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible', a false ending on 'Last Bus Home: Lights Out on Progress Street', and the secret 5:26 1980s Stadium Rock (AOR) closing reclamation of 'Electric Color'.
