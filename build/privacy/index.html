Privacy Policy

Effective Across music.raggiesoft.com & the RaggieSoft Media Network • Operated by Michael P. Ragsdale

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Listening Room (music.raggiesoft.com), part of the RaggieSoft.com network operated by Michael P. Ragsdale. I respect your privacy and have engineered this Progressive Web App to collect the absolute minimum information required to deliver audio streams, synchronized lyrics, and lore. There are no user accounts, no login walls, and no invasive tracking profiles on this app.

2. Information We Collect & Store

A. Local Browser Storage (100% On-Device)

To provide a seamless listening experience without requiring an account, The Listening Room uses your browser’s local localStorage and Progressive Web App Service Worker cache to remember:

  • Your last played track, queue, and timestamp (raggiesoft_music_session_v1) so you can resume where you left off.
  • Your preferred default streaming service (raggiesoft_preferred_dsp — Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, or YouTube Music).
  • Your visual theme, color mode, and accessibility settings (engine_room_settings).
  • Dismissed notification banners so we don’t repeatedly interrupt your session.

This data stays strictly inside your own browser and is never transmitted to our servers. You can reset or clear it at any time from the Settings page.

B. Server Logs & Asset Delivery

Like most web servers, our primary server (“Jessica”) and our CDN (assets.raggiesoft.com) automatically log standard HTTP requests to /var/log/nginx/access.log. This includes your IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, and timestamp. These logs are retained briefly for security auditing, bandwidth diagnostics, and debugging, and are never sold or shared with third-party data brokers.

C. Infrastructure & Edge Security (Cloudflare)

This website utilizes Cloudflare as a Content Delivery Network (CDN) and reverse-proxy security perimeter. Cloudflare processes network traffic (including IP addresses and system fingerprints) to protect the site from DDoS attacks and malicious bots, and may set strictly necessary security cookies (such as __cf_bm) to verify human traffic.

D. E-Commerce & Digital Album Storefront (Fourthwall)

Our official digital download and merchandise store at store.raggiesoft.com is hosted and operated by Fourthwall, who acts as the legal Merchant of Record. When you purchase a High-Quality MP3/OGG archive, Studio Master WAV/FLAC archive, or physical merchandise, you transact directly with Fourthwall.

RaggieSoft receives basic order summaries to monitor store performance, but we never collect, process, or store your credit card or payment details on our servers.

Read Fourthwall’s Privacy Policy ↗

3. External Streaming Services & Third-Party Links

The Listening Room provides direct links to stream our catalog on external Digital Service Providers (Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music). Once you click an external streaming link and leave music.raggiesoft.com, your activity on that platform is governed by the privacy policy of that respective streaming service.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the contact methods listed at raggiesoft.com/contact ↗.
